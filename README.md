##  Overview

**SkyLink Customer Churn Analysis** is an interactive **Power BI business intelligence project** focused on understanding customer churn and identifying the factors that influence customer retention.

The dashboard transforms customer data into actionable insights by analyzing churn across different customer segments, contract types, account lengths, service usage, charges, and customer-service interactions.

The project is designed to help answer questions such as:

- Which customer groups have the highest churn?
- How does contract type relate to churn?
- Does customer account length affect churn?
- How do customer-service calls relate to customer retention?
- How do extra data and international charges vary across age groups?
- Which factors are the strongest influencers of customer behavior and churn?

---

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze customer churn patterns.
- Identify customer segments with higher churn rates.
- Compare churn across contract types and age groups.
- Investigate the relationship between account length and churn.
- Analyze customer-service call activity.
- Explore extra data and international charges.
- Use Power BI's **Key Influencers** and interactive filtering to identify important drivers.
- Present the results through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard.

---

##  Dashboard Analysis

The Power BI dashboard contains multiple views for exploring customer behavior and churn.

### Customer Churn

- Churned vs. non-churned customers.
- Churn distribution across age categories.
- Churn by contract type.
- Churn rate by customer groups.
- Churn rate across account length.

### Customer Behavior

- Average monthly direct-debit customers.
- Average monthly customer-service calls.
- Customer-service calls by contract type.
- Customer activity and account characteristics.

### Charges & Usage

- Extra data charges by age.
- Extra international charges by age.
- Extra data charges for unlimited data-plan customers.
- Comparison of charges across different customer segments.

### Key Influencers

Power BI's **Key Influencers** visual is used to investigate variables that influence customer behavior, including average monthly data usage and other customer characteristics.
